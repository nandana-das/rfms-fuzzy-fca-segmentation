# Version 2 (hybrid): exploratory results

> **EXPLORATORY, POST HOC: version 2, chosen after seeing v1.** This study was motivated by a v1 result and evaluated on the same origins, so its gains are optimistic and need confirmation on new data. It **does not replace or modify** the frozen v1 methodology, results, hypotheses (H1–H3) or conclusions (commit `7607e09`; `docs/METHODOLOGY_FINAL.md`, `docs/RESULTS_SUMMARY.md`). Source: `results/v2_hybrid/`, generated by `scripts/v2_hybrid_ladder.py` in one run.

**Integrity check.** The five arms shared with v1 (raw, log, spline, crisp RFM-FCA, fuzzy RFM-FCA) reproduce the frozen v1 values exactly (maximum absolute difference 0.0).

**The change.** Features are the v1 fuzzy concept memberships (unchanged) plus standardized log1p(R, F, M), fitted on the training folds. Nothing was tuned.

## Interpretation (exploratory)

- **Saturation.** Adding log magnitude removes the v1 top-band saturation shortfall on Online Retail II.
- **What the concepts add.**
  - P4 shows that the concept memberships add predictive information beyond *linear* log RFM.
  - It does **not** show that concepts add value beyond a generic nonlinear transformation of RFM. The spline baseline is that comparison (P1): the hybrid is only marginally better on Online Retail II and not significantly different on Dunnhumby.
  - The evidence is therefore consistent with the concepts acting mainly as a nonlinear (and interpretable) encoding of RFM, rather than as an independent source of predictive value.
- **Fuzzy vs crisp.** In P3 the hybrid with fuzzy concepts beats the hybrid with crisp concepts on the regression targets in both datasets, consistent with v1's H1.
- **Post hoc caveat.** The margins over the spline (+0.0032 to +0.0055 on Online Retail II) are of the same order as the optimism a post hoc design can introduce. v2 should be reported as an exploratory follow-up needing confirmation on new data.
- **Scope.** The hybrid's segments are identical to v1's, so its segment stability equals v1's (see `docs/V2_STABILITY_RESULTS.md`); prediction stability was not evaluated. No v1 hypothesis status changes.
