## 2. Key Methodological Takeaways from the Base Paper

1. **Unstated Tie-Breaking Rule:**  
   The base paper states that customers were partitioned into five equal quintiles, but does not state how ties were broken. On Online Retail II, 1,623 customers (27.61%) have raw frequency $F=1$, which forces at least 448 customers with $F=1$ into quintile 2. Because the tie-handling rule is omitted, exact concept customer count matching is mathematically impossible without the authors' private script.
2. **FCA Was Never Evaluated on Clustering Metrics:**  
   Rungruang et al. explicitly classify FCA as **soft overlapping clustering** and K-means/Ward as **hard non-overlapping clustering** (Table 10). They computed Silhouette and Davies-Bouldin **strictly for K-means and Ward** to select $k$, and **never computed Silhouette or Davies-Bouldin for FCA**.
3. **Lineage of Non-Binary / Fuzzy FCA:**  
   The authors explicitly conclude Section 6 by proposing non-binary formal contexts as future work. Our fuzzy extension directly fulfills this proposed research vector.
