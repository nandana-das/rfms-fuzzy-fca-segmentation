# Consolidated predictive evidence: fuzzy RFM-FCA

Generated by `scripts/build_consolidated_evidence.py` from saved artifacts only (no model refit). Every number is read from the source CSV named in its section; the builder re-checks each source's Holm family and pooled deltas and re-parses this report against the sources.

What that verification covers: numerical traceability of every reported number, interval and Holm p-value to a saved output, and agreement of each significance label with its test family. During preparation a separate parse of this report against the raw CSVs (372 numbers, 0 mismatches) was also run; that checker is not part of the repository. Neither check validates the experimental methodology, the soundness of the source pipelines, or the interpretive statements, which rest on the analyses and limitations described in each section.

Scope: out-of-sample prediction (repurchase AUC, log future spend R², log future invoice count R²) on rolling origins with 91-day holdouts. Segment stability (H3), the CDNOW confirmation, and the v2 hybrid are covered by `docs/PAPER_EVIDENCE_MAP.md` and are not re-consolidated here, except the CDNOW rows of the five-seed robustness analysis.

## 4. Temporal attributes (T = mean inter-purchase interval, L = observed tenure)

Definitions: T is the mean gap between consecutive distinct purchase dates up to the origin; customers with fewer than two dates get the training-fold median T plus `repeat_history` = 0. L is days from first observed purchase to the origin (left-censored by the data start). All features, imputation, memberships, mining, suppression and learners are fit inside training folds; outcomes use the following 91-day holdout.

## 8. Limitations

- Two retail datasets for most analyses (three for Section 2); three outcomes; linear learners for all non-GBM arms; one untuned GBM.
- Rolling origins share customers (Dunnhumby: 2,497 of 2,499 households in all four origins). Sections 3, 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 6 pool independent per-origin bootstraps, so their intervals are approximate; where checked (4.4), the household-cluster intervals were nearly identical.
- Bootstrap intervals reflect test-sample variability; only Section 2 also reflects refitting over CV seeds, and 4.3 checks two fold assignments.
- Several Holm p-values sit at the bootstrap floor of their family (e.g. 0.0135 for the 27-test ladder); they bound significance, not effect size.
- Not significant is not equivalence.
- Post hoc analyses (4.2, 4.3 comparison choice, 4.4, 5) are hypothesis-generating.
- Numerical traceability (see the note under the title) is not methodological validation.
