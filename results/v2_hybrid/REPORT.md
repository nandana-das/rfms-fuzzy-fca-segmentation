## Protocol

- Dunnhumby origins (day cutoffs): [347, 438, 529, 620]; Online Retail II origins: ['2010-09-10', '2010-12-10', '2011-03-11', '2011-06-10', '2011-09-09']; holdout = next 91 days.
- Online Retail II primary protocol (2010-12-09, 365-day holdout) is reported per origin but excluded from POOLED.
- Stratified 5-fold CV over customers within each origin; all representations fit on training folds.
- Paired customer-level bootstrap (B=2000); Holm correction over the pooled comparison family per dataset.
- Bootstrap intervals reflect test-sample variability only; origins overlap, so pooled intervals are approximate.
- Scoring: tie-preserving quintiles fit on training folds (identical raw values share a band).
